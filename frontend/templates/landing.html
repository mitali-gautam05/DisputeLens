How a case moves through the system

01

Classify

A risk model scores every transaction's likelihood of becoming a chargeback, using behavioral and transaction features.

02

Retrieve & draft

For flagged cases, relevant evidence is retrieved and a response letter is drafted with inline citations back to that evidence.

03

Review & act

Nothing is auto-submitted. High-confidence drafts wait for review, medium cases require sign-off, low-risk cases are simply logged.

Reading a case

High risk

Auto-drafted response, awaiting review before it's used.

Medium risk

Draft generated, but requires explicit human sign-off.

Low risk

No draft generated — logged to the audit trail only.
